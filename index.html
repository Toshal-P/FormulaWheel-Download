🚀 How To Get Started

1

Download & Extract the Windows Receiver

Download the PC Receiver zip, extract it on your Windows computer, and run TSteerReceiver.exe. (Requires free ViGEmBus driver).

2

Install FormulaWheel on your Android Phone

Install FormulaWheel.apk on your phone. Connect via USB cable with USB Debugging enabled, or connect to the same Wi-Fi.

3

Configure F1 Telemetry & Race!

In your game settings (F1 2018/2020/etc.), turn UDP Telemetry ON, set Port to 20777 and Format to 2018 or 2020. Hit the track!
